Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Overview

Poincare Sketchpad is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer. Your privacy is straightforward: the app collects nothing.

Data Collection

Poincare Sketchpad does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No network requests of any kind
  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No cookies or local tracking identifiers

Data Storage

All diagram files you create are saved locally on your computer in the location you choose. The app does not upload files to any server.

Third-Party Services

Poincare Sketchpad does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit the Support page.
